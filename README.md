##  Plot Summary

> “What happens when your childhood frenemy becomes your next-door neighbor?”

**Choi Seung Hyo**, a brilliant young architect, and **Bae Seok Ryu**, a successful project manager, were once childhood acquaintances — forced into awkward playdates by their mothers. Years later, fate brings them back together as neighbors. What begins as a series of comedic misunderstandings slowly evolves into a heartfelt romance, as they rediscover each other and confront their shared past.
